Connect your world to Darkride

Linking this server to a world on your Darkride account lets your world be listed in Places, so people can find it and come in.

  1. Press Connect to Darkride. Your Darkride worlds open in a new tab.
  2. Make a world there, or pick one you have, and copy its access token.
  3. Come back to this tab and paste the token in.

Who can come in, and who can build

You can do anything here from this computer: anyone using the computer the server runs on is an administrator.

Add your own Darkride username to manage your world from other computers too. Separate names with commas.

Everyone else gets what you choose here. You can change it any time in Settings.
